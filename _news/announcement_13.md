We had the pleasure of welcoming **Kayo Yoshida, Consul for Economic Affairs at the Consulate-General of Japan in Rio de Janeiro**, and **Rebecca Souza, Economic Affairs Advisor**, to the Institute of Computing at UFF. During the visit, we presented our institute, graduate program, and ongoing research in **Artificial Intelligence**, including efficient LLM training, AI for finance and social well-being, ethical and sustainable AI, extreme rainfall prediction, citizen-centered AI, and diffusion models for engineering applications. 
The visit provided a valuable opportunity to exchange perspectives and explore future research collaborations between Brazil and Japan.
