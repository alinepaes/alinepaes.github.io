BRAGFOST 2026 – AI for Good
September 24–27, 2026 

I had the pleasure of participating in the 16th Brazilian-German Frontiers of Science and Technology Symposium (BRAGFOST 2026), held in Goiânia, Brazil, under the theme “AI for Good: Global Responsible Innovation and Business Transformation.” I served as co-chair, alongside Sabine Weber, of the session “Trustworthy AI in the Age of LLMs.” The event brought together researchers from Brazil and Germany for interdisciplinary discussions and scientific exchange. 
Many thanks to the Alexander von Humboldt Foundation, CAPES, and CEIA-UFG for making this event possible.
