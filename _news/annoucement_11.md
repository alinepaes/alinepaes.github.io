On August 12, I had the great pleasure of speaking at the First BPLN Khipu School on Natural Language Processing. 
My talk explored large language models (LLMs): where they come from, how they work, and how they are evaluated.

It was a wonderful opportunity to share ideas, discuss the opportunities and limitations of these models, and connect with 
students and researchers interested in natural language processing. I would like to thank the organizers for the invitation 
and everyone who participated in the session and contributed thoughtful questions.
